# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming place for musicians, developers, reverse
engineers, and first-time contributors.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and disagree with
ideas without attacking people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
sexualized conduct, and publishing another person's private data are not
acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns
privately to the maintainers through the repository's available private contact
channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

By participating, you agree to follow these expectations in repository issues,
pull requests, discussions, and project-related communication.
